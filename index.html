Our Process

The Trulander Transaction Process.

The exact steps every buyer goes through with us — nothing skipped, nothing rushed.

Talk To Our Team

An initial conversation with our sales team, then we schedule your free site visit to Royal Palm Enclave.

Independent Land Search

We hand you everything you need to verify the land yourself at the Lands Commission — no pressure, no rush.

Quotation & Contract

Once you're satisfied and accept your quotation, formal registration forms and the contract for sale are signed.

Secure Payment

Payment goes directly to Trulander's accounts only — never cash — with a receipt issued for every payment made.

Indenture Prepared

Indenture preparation begins once your 50% deposit is in, and is ready within 1–10 days.

Land Allocated

Your plot is allocated at 50% payment — you can start your project right after the initial deposit.

About Us — Who Are We?

We are the land experts.

Trulander JSF Limited is a registered real estate company, and a GREDA (Ghana Real Estate Developers Association) member. At Trulander, we aim to provide genuine land offerings, accessible and available for all. Every property we market and sell goes through rigorous due diligence to ensure it's void of any foreseeable risk — so you can acquire land for your dream home or project in peace.

To help humanity prosper by providing property void of risk, at your convenience.

Our Vision
