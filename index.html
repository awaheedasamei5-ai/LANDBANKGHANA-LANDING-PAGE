How We Help

Help is the whole point.

Land in Ghana can be confusing and risky to navigate alone. LandBank Ghana exists to stand between you and every bad deal — with real verification, real people, and real follow-through.

Due Diligence & Title Search

We investigate every title at the Lands Commission, search court & family records for disputes, and confirm boundaries before you spend a cedi.

Litigation-Free Acquisition

We only guide you toward land we've verified is free of family disputes, double sales, or court litigation — so what you buy stays yours.

Support for Buyers

From shortlisting the right plot to registration and documentation, we walk with you to a safe, stress-free handover.

Support for Sellers

Selling land safely takes more than a signboard. We help you package, verify, and connect your land with serious, ready buyers.

Our Process

How we take you from inquiry to handover.

A clear, five-step process built around one goal: no surprises after you've paid.

Tell Us What You Need

Share your budget, purpose, and preferred location — residential, commercial, or investment.

We Search & Verify

Lands Commission search, family/court records check, and boundary confirmation on every shortlisted plot.

You Visit, We Confirm

An accompanied site visit where we walk the actual boundaries with you, in person.

Documentation & Registration

Final handover with every document filed and copies in your hands.
